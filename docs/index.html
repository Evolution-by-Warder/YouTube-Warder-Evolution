Google account sign-in / Prihlásenie cez Google

YouTube Warder Evolution uses the Google OAuth 2.0 TV and limited-input device flow. Signing in is optional. If you authorize it, the extension requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly permission only to read YouTube account data such as subscriptions and associated channel metadata. It does not request permission to upload, edit or delete videos.

Prihlásenie do účtu Google je dobrovoľné. Doplnok používa autorizáciu pre televízne zariadenia a žiada výhradne oprávnenie na čítanie údajov YouTube, najmä osobných odberov.

Learn how information is handled in our Privacy Policy / Zásady ochrany súkromia. Read the Terms of Use / Podmienky používania before connecting your account.

Contact / Kontakt

Questions about the project or Google sign-in? Contact the maintainers via the YouTube Warder Evolution issue tracker. Please do not publish passwords, OAuth codes or tokens in public issues.
