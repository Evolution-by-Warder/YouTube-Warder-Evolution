YouTube Warder Evolution

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · Applies to YouTube Warder Evolution for Enigma2

1. Who provides the app?

YouTube Warder Evolution is an independent Enigma2 community plugin maintained through the Evolution-by-Warder GitHub repository. The plugin is not an official Google or YouTube product. Contact: project issue tracker (do not share private credentials there).

2. Optional YouTube account access

Public YouTube browsing does not require signing in. If you connect your account using Google's TV/device authorization page, the plugin uses the YouTube permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube to read your subscriptions and liked videos. Only when you explicitly choose the Like action for a selected video does the plugin send the corresponding rating to YouTube. It does not use this function to upload or delete videos.

3. Information stored on your receiver

The plugin stores Google OAuth access/refresh tokens locally on the receiver so that you can remain signed in. The current OAuth token file is stored under /etc/enigma2/warderyoutube-account-token.json with restricted permissions (0600). An optional receiver-local client configuration can be stored in /etc/enigma2/warderyoutube-account-client.json. Previous account-token files may remain to support a rollback; the plugin does not publish them. Local video favourites and viewing history are stored in /etc/enigma2/warderyoutube-library.json. These files are not part of the public website. The plugin does not ask for or store your Google password.

4. Use, transmission and sharing

The plugin uses authorized account data to display subscriptions and liked videos. Missing liked videos may be copied one-way into the receiver-local Favourites collection on app opening; this sync does not transmit your receiver-local favourites back to YouTube. A direct Like action sends only the selected video rating. Authorized requests go from the receiver to Google's YouTube API and OAuth services; video playback and public content lookup require connecting to the relevant external services. We do not operate a Warder Evolution account-data collection server for this plugin, and the plugin does not intentionally upload your Google OAuth tokens or personal subscriptions to a Warder Evolution server. We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

5. Retention and deletion

OAuth credentials and local favourites/history remain on your receiver until you remove them or reset the relevant settings. Using the plugin's disconnect-account feature removes saved account tokens and attempts to revoke Google authorization. You can also withdraw access through Google account connections. You may delete the local library/history in the plugin's controls where available, or manually remove the corresponding file from the receiver. An uninstall may preserve user configuration by design; uninstalling alone is not a guarantee that local data was erased.

6. Third-party services and website hosting

Google and YouTube independently process information according to their policies: Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service. This informational website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process standard website access information according to its privacy statement. We do not use this website to collect your Google tokens.

7. Security and updates

Account tokens are stored in owner-accessible local files (0600) using atomic replacement, without your Google password. YouTube account requests use HTTPS; bearer tokens are sent in authorization headers rather than URLs and are not intentionally included in application logs. The plugin rejects cross-host redirects for authorized requests. Local Favourites and History use private local storage and preserve existing entries during one-way synchronization. Protect your receiver and backups, and keep the plugin updated. Changes to these practices are published here.

8. Questions

Contact the maintainers through GitHub Issues. For sensitive matters, do not disclose tokens, passwords or personal account data in public posts.

Zásady ochrany súkromia – slovenské zhrnutie

YouTube Warder Evolution je nezávislý doplnok Enigma2. Prihlásenie cez Google je voliteľné. Po udelení súhlasu žiada iba oprávnenie youtube.readonly, aby na prijímači zobrazoval osobné odbery YouTube a súvisiace informácie. Nevyžaduje právo meniť obsah účtu.

Prístupové tokeny sú uložené iba lokálne v prijímači, spolu s lokálnymi obľúbenými položkami a históriou. Vývojári neprevádzkujú server, ktorý by tieto údaje zhromažďoval. Požiadavky na YouTube smerujú priamo z prijímača do služieb Google/YouTube. Údaje nepredávame ani nevyužívame na reklamu.

Prístup je možné odpojiť v plugine a odvolať v nastaveniach účtu Google. Pri odinštalovaní môžu zostať zachované nastavenia; podľa potreby vymaž aj lokálne súbory. Otázky nám môžeš poslať cez GitHub Issues, bez zverejňovania hesiel a tokenov. Podrobnosti sú uvedené v anglickej verzii vyššie.
