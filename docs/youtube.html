YouTube Warder Evolution

An independent, community-developed YouTube browsing and playback plugin for compatible Enigma2 television receivers running systems such as OpenATV and OpenPLi.

Nezávislý komunitný YouTube plugin pre prijímače Enigma2. Ponúka vyhľadávanie, prehrávanie videí, kategórie, obľúbené položky a voliteľné osobné odbery.

Public source code and releasesPrivacy Policy

Video discovery & playback

Browse video results and categories, search for videos, inspect details and play compatible videos directly on the receiver. Public features do not require connecting a Google account.

Optional YouTube subscriptions

Authorize access to your own YouTube account via Google's TV and limited-input device sign-in flow to view your subscriptions and related channel and video information on the receiver. OAuth is optional.

Favourites & history

Keep receiver-local favourites and playback history. While signed in, the plugin can import missing YouTube liked videos into local favourites and lets you explicitly Like a video in its MENU.

Why does the app request Google access?

Account sign-in uses Google's TV device page. The app requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube to read real subscriptions and liked videos. The action Like on YouTube changes the rating of a video only when explicitly selected by the user; automatic one-way favourites import does not send local favourites back to YouTube.

Google supplies the authorization code and token directly to the device-flow client. The app does not request your Google password. Access and refresh tokens are stored locally on your receiver with restricted file permissions; there is no developer-hosted account-token server. You can disconnect your account in the plugin and revoke permission in Google Account connections.
